# Privacy, custody and operation

These criteria are part of the product and its acceptances. The presence of ZK tests, different keys or encrypted at rest does not in itself demonstrate system privacy or independent operators.

## Current borders

| Componente | Information or authority you receive today | Limit of acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet web local | Private view of the owner and original manual laboratory permissions | Does not prove native custody on a phone |
| Mobile Wallet | Key secp256k1 protected by operating system; revised permissions and pending operation | The key temporarily enters JavaScript memory; pending device isolation and recovery |
| Mobile API | Stable public key, origin, action, network, asset, amount, destination, commission, ID and expiration | Can correlate owner and operations; does not receive private key |
| platform postgreSQL | Challenges, terms and admission results, with durable single consumption | `ADMITTED` does not credit reserve, execution or settlement |
| Original local workers | Private witnesses and statements of job by role by IPC | The worker and his host see the witness; the separation of processes does not hide that information from the operator |
| Native adapters and PRCs | Public transactions, amounts, destinations, times, connections and finality | Your observations require checking identity and canonical chain; a missing response does not equal zero |
| Operators and laboratory executor | Separate original authorities and synthetic liquidity of the solver | A single host is still an assumption of common trust |

The platform API starts with your economic executor without connecting. Real availability requires healthy persistence and a proven original adapter. Public contract `privatePortfolio: null` while the authenticated private view is missing. An owner view should not be displayed by means of an publicly available endpoint.

## What each integration should keep

1. Custody authorizes explicit limits on the original ID. A challenge
mobile does not replace the signature of a Noir frame or enable laboratory capability. The economic bridge must verify both borders.
2. The journal records the attempt before the first external effect. After a crash
or lost response, the original is observed. Do not create another nonce, transaction or payment to resolve an unknown result.
3. Confirming requires transaction, terms, canonical inclusion, backing and deltas
protocol. An available balance is never invented from a log, quote or public position.
4. A recovery respects the native deliveries that have already ended. The report
must show returned reserve, changes, external delivery and separate loss/obligation of the solver; recover a note does not undo a transfer.
5. Root keys and private witnesses do not belong to the Terraform, image
public, HTTP log, public traceback or variables `EXPO_PUBLIC_*`. A test job on Cloud requires a delivery/time contract and deletion of the witness; that Cloud transport is not yet implemented.

## Privacy acceptance pending

Define and check separate observers: API operator, worker, RPC, public chain, storage service, network and device. Include collusion and link funding, authorisation, execution, exit and recovery by amount, time, public identity and pattern of queries. The amounts and public keys of the current mobile contract are visible to the API.

The transportation and credentials of the future private state require permissions for role and separation of access. Removing quantities of logs limits exposure, but does not prevent the service from seeing the messages you need to process. No anonymity is attributed to the current client or to a centralized worker.

Independent cryptographic review of circuits, verifiers, framing, fixed sources and witness management is needed. Internal tests and synthetic laboratories do not replace that review.

## Performance and availability

The integrated acceptance must publish hardware, versions, load and separate measures of initialization, witness, test, verification, coordination, observation and native finality. Store results cold and hot, medium, 95 percentile and maximum. The number of correct tests does not measure latency.

The original hot targets are total self-costing less than 30 s, tests less than 20 s and coordination/liquidation less than 5 s, with separate native finality and without artificial waits. Compilation diagnoses or ZK-free transfers do not demonstrate these objectives.

The current EVM preparation starts Barretenberg to generate/verify the VK; its start time should not be hidden as hot performance. Reusing a worker requires retaining the exact association of job, role, statement and VK, and removing witnesses between jobs. Checks will not be reduced to improve the figure.

Operation Cloud is being prepared locally. There is no GCP project or deployment yet. Images, permissions, secrets, persistence, backups, limits and readiness require validation of the real service before enabling funds. Proof jobs remain disabled until such integration is available.
