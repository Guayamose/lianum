# Roadmap

The core implementation is substantial: a mobile wallet, persistent agent, API gateway, PAEG graph execution, genuine proof generation, settlement, recovery and deployment tooling. Delivery work should close the remaining evidence and usability gaps in a controlled sequence.

1. **Finish release acceptance.** The recorded V47 engine reached READY and its public routes passed. Source4's original stopped-plan withdrawal is now confirmed on the phone and independently finalized on Sepolia. The next plan is still blocked by the cumulative source quota of four: the owner-approved increase to five was attempted through the live control, but rejected because the Source journal has not refreshed the withdrawal from CONFIRMED to FINALIZED. Resolve that observation/CAS boundary without rewriting history, then verify the successor handoff, public payment graph, settlement and executor payout. The repository also contains a verified portable CRS cache-path fix that has not been deployed. Complete public Uniswap, Aave and Morpho round trips and record their exact results. Keep uncertain operations pending until their original outcome is known.
2. **Close wallet accounting gaps.** Complete personal-wallet funding into PAEG, incoming activity and combined private/DeFi valuation. Do not substitute server-sponsored demo funding for a personal deposit in the product description.
3. **Verify the English mobile experience.** Check onboarding, Mainnet/Testnet selection, balances, native sends, plan review, recovery, agent streaming, voice and notifications on the phone. Keep technical diagnostics in advanced views while retaining financial terms in every review.
4. **Package a reproducible handoff.** Commit the real source, provide the short English documentation route, retain detailed references and publish only sanitized evidence. Record required external resources and configuration so a reviewer can distinguish fast checks from full financial laboratories.
5. **Complete submission assets.** Align the final deck and video with verified capabilities and the required delivery format. Presentation working files and large media are kept separately from ordinary source history.
6. **Extend after the delivery baseline.** Add authenticated provisioning of fresh targets for repeated protocol use; V35 source continuation preserves existing targets and does not reset their limits. Add fiat onboarding such as MoonPay, broader agent strategies, more network/protocol coverage and measured latency improvements. Production mainnet expansion requires its own operational and security acceptance.

A UI or documentation change does not require restarting the engine. Group financial fixes with their focused regressions, then deploy a reviewed release and verify the affected public behavior. Previous results retain their original generation and scope.
